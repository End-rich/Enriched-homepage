About

I am a second-year master's student in mathematics at the University of Windsor, working with ADVISOR NAME. Before coming to Windsor I studied in Korea.

I am interested in homotopical and higher-categorical foundations: model categories and localisation, $\infty$-categories, operads and $A_\infty$-structures, and derived algebraic geometry. I am currently applying to PhD programmes.

Languages: Korean (native), English, Japanese.

Research

My work sits at the interface of higher category theory and derived algebraic geometry. Broadly, I like questions that can be phrased in terms of descent and localisation rather than explicit algebra or geometry, and I enjoy the model-categorical side of the subject.
